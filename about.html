About me

I keep systems running - servers, automation and small scripts that reduce manual work. I aim for reliable, maintainable solutions, favour clear logging and predictable behavior, and try to avoid unnecessary complexity.

Journey

I started my IT journey in elementary school, making game projects on Scratch and teaching myself HTML, JavaScript and CSS - those early experiments taught me iteration and systems thinking. I later completed technical school in 2025: Informatikai rendszer- és alkalmazás üzemeltető technikus (vocational diploma - IT Systems & Applications Operations Technician), where I focused on system administration, networking and applied tooling. I also explored university coursework at Óbuda University (NIK), studying toward a BSc in Engineering Informatics, but chose to return to hands-on projects because I learn fastest by building and iterating in the real world.

My background mixes formal training with self-directed projects: home servers, small-scale networks, device repair, and automation that keeps services reliable without constant babysitting.

What I work on

My practical focus is backend and systems work: service orchestration, game-server hosting, and small services and bridges (for example, Discord integrations). I also do hardware-level tinkering - Raspberry Pi/Arduino projects, maintenance and repair when it makes sense.

Project highlights

Home-server orchestration

I run and maintain a multi-service home server that hosts game instances, bots and personal projects. I focus on reproducible deployment and monitoring so services stay online with minimal intervention. More projects

Discord bridge & automation

Small tooling that connects services and automates routine tasks - status bridges, alerts and lightweight bots. These are practical tools: small, well-logged, and designed to fail gracefully.

Outside of work

I prefer solo hobbies where I can practice, iterate and be better than yesterday. I’m into pixel art and DJ mixing - both satisfy the same systems-oriented part of my brain: constraints, iteration and pattern. I enjoy exploring other hobbies that fit that mindset and am always trying new things to see what clicks.

Personality (INTJ‑T)

I identify as INTJ‑T. In practical terms that means I’m strategic, analytical and private by default, though I’m friendly and I try my best in social interactions. I prefer written, specific communication and clear goals over vague discussion. Strengths include systems thinking, long-term planning, and a focus on efficient, reliable solutions. I can sometimes come across as blunt or skip formalities, but I aim to be considerate and clear in collaboration.

Values

I support the right-to-repair movement and follow creators like Salem Techsperts - practical repair and preservation matter to me. I value competence, autonomy and tools that empower sustained use over disposable convenience.
